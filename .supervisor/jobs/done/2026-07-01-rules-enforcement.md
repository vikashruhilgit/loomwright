## Task
**Goal:** Wire the committed `.agent/rules/` house-rules store into the workflow as **ADVISORY enforcement** at three seams, and **mechanize + gate** the two security-sensitive paths the substrate (#3b-i) deliberately shipped as prose — closing the `rules` skill's §9 (unattended-`check` trust valve) and §9.1 (`/rules add` mechanization) inherited-requirement notes.

**Problem Statement:**
Slice #3b-i shipped the substrate: a committed store, a fail-safe reader (`read-rules.sh`) that NEVER executes a `check`, and the `/rules` authoring command. But (a) nothing reads those rules into the workflow yet — an implementer still can't see conventions on the DO side (the whole point of Bet 6 — conventions were enforced only on the REVIEW side), and (b) the two write/execute paths remain unmechanized prose: `/rules add`'s path-containment (the reviewer's TOP inheritance callout — a typo/hostile `category` is contained only by an agent faithfully following prose) and `/rules check`'s execution (no default-off gate exists for an unattended caller). This slice wires the reader advisorily at the worker / Phase 4.5 / SessionStart seams (mirroring the `prior_churn` advisory contract — never gates, never changes `heal_decision`, empty=proceed), mechanizes `/rules add` into a sole-writer `add-rule.sh` with a real traversal-rejection test, and mechanizes `/rules check` into `rules-check.sh` with a `--no-cmd`-style trust valve so no unattended/enforcement seam can ever run author-supplied shell without an explicit gate.

This is north-star **slice #3b-ii** (the enforcement half of Bet 6). See `loomwright/docs/SPIKES/NORTH_STAR_DIRECTION.md` §"6. House rules" and the `rules` skill §9/§9.1 deferral notes. Follows #3b-i (`/rules` substrate, PR #84). Division per the doc: **`/setup twin` bootstraps; `/rules` maintains** — this slice makes the maintained rules *visible* (advisory) without ever making them *gate*.
