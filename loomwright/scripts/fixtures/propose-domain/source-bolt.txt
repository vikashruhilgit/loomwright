Bolt Knowledge - platform overview (stand-in for fetched source text; committed, never fetched
over a network)

Bolt Knowledge is a documentation and search platform for engineering organisations.

Security and administration
  - Two-factor authentication is required for every administrator, with an authenticator app
    or a hardware key.
  - Role-based access is enforced per space: roles and permissions are inherited by child
    pages and can be overridden per document.
  - A self-serve bulk export lets an administrator export to CSV or JSON at any time; the
    data export runs without contacting support.
  - Where a customer buys through the marketplace, cardholder data is held in a PCI-DSS
    tokenized card vault operated by the payment processor, never by Bolt.

Nothing else on this page is a capability claim.
