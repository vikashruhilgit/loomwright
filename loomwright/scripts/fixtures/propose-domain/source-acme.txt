Acme Docs - product page (stand-in for fetched source text; committed, never fetched over a network)

Acme Docs is a hosted knowledge base for engineering teams.

Platform
  - The public API applies per-client rate limiting on every endpoint, with the remaining
    budget returned on each response.
  - Every space keeps an audit log of who read, edited, moved or deleted a page, retained
    for one year.
  - Single sign-on is standard on every plan: SAML and OpenID Connect against your own
    identity provider, with just-in-time provisioning.
  - Outbound webhooks fire on page publish, comment and archive events, with retries and a
    replayable delivery history.

Nothing else on this page is a capability claim.
