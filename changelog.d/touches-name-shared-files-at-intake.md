Product Owner names the shared files a story plugs into
The Product Owner's `## Touches` used to come from its own file-impact reasoning alone, so a story listed the new files it creates and missed the existing ones it must register in (an index, a policy table, kernel wiring, the test that pins the feature list); in loomwright-studio two items declared 11 and 10 files and needed 26 and 25, giving half-size `right-size` verdicts and lanes that found half their files at Launch Pad (#808). Responsibilities §"2. Requirement Definition" of `agents/product-owner.md` and its `commands/product-owner.md` mirror now carry a **Shared-file sweep:** bullet: for a story that adds a symbol, route, setting or category, find an existing similar feature and list the existing files that register, dispatch, configure or test it; with no similar feature, say so in Assumptions; skip a file a `.agent/companions.json` rule already adds. A contract change the sweep finds goes in the Acceptance Criteria, or, when its required form is unknown, becomes a new contract-change flag raised at the existing Pre-persist (3a) gate under `PO-ASSUME` and counted in `po_gate: needs_owner (<n> flags)` — no new question gate. `skills/user-story-writing/SKILL.md` gains the matching sweep bullet pointing writers at a `.agent/companions.json` rule (proposed to the owner, never written) for shared files every story of a kind needs. `test-requirement-shape.sh` pins it as (S7) with seven mutation controls. Prompt budgets re-measured, none raised.
