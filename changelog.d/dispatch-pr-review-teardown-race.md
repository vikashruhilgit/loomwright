test-dispatch-pr-review.sh waits for the whole teardown trap before reading `.died`
The detached review-drain wrapper removes its worktree and then its lock, and only after that does death detection write, or not write, the `.died` marker. The suite's `wait_for_teardown` returned once the lock was gone, so on a slow runner the death-detection legs read `.died` before it existed: `main` went red at 876aa7d with `attempt1=''` on a tree that had passed on its PR. The absent-marker legs, including case 42's BLOCKING mutation control, could also pass vacuously. `wait_for_teardown` now also waits until no live process carries the worktree path in its argv, because the wrapper exits only after the trap's last write. Its budget is 30 s. Cases 37, 38, 39/40/41 and 42 fail on a teardown timeout instead of swallowing it with `|| true`. No dispatcher change.
