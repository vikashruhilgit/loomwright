Drain dismissals carry checkable evidence
Every finding the `/review-pr --until-mergeable` drain dismisses now carries one line of evidence the owner can check in seconds: a repo `path:line`, a commit sha, or a double-quoted span from the PR diff, PR body or brief. `review-heal-next-step.sh --result` refuses a dismissal without one (exit 2, `--result: a dismissed finding needs evidence …`, nothing written, the pending spawn re-prints), checked by one jq `def evidence_ok` after the existing malformed and id checks so every earlier refusal keeps its message. The check is form only: the driver does not look the path, sha or quote up, so a plausible but wrong citation still passes and is the owner's to overturn at the park. A below-floor item's evidence is the driver's own (`severity <SEV> is below the fix floor <FLOOR>`). When the proof lives outside the repo (an owner decision, a convention), the validate and fallback spawns tell the validator to report the finding `confirmed` with `auto_fixable: false`, so it lands in `needs_human`, still blocks READY and reaches the owner. The evidence shows on an indented `  - evidence:` line under the unchanged marker-comment bullet (the postmortem parser still reads only the bullets), as `evidence:` on the `REVIEW_HEAL_RESULT` `dismissed` item (a new optional key of the drain item only; Phase 4.5's `heal_dismissed` is unchanged), and as `- **Evidence:** …` under `- **Reason dismissed:**` in `/automate`'s dismissed-findings draft, which reads `none recorded` for a result written before this change and for every Phase 4.5 item. Evidence never feeds a draft's name or any ledger decision.
