Plan-time house-rule conflicts are surfaced, never silently overridden (#432, plan half)
Before this change, a requirement that contradicted an advisory house rule went through planning unflagged. Plan Reviewer Criterion 17 gave an advisory rule at most a LOW note, Product Owner never read the rules store, and a brief or spawn prompt could call the violation "out of scope". Product Owner has a new Pre-persist house-rule check, after the stories are drafted and before anything is persisted, in both modes. It runs `read-rules.sh --with-ids` over the story's Touches paths, which is read-only and never executes a rule's `check`. Every AC that contradicts a rule is written to a `## Rule conflicts (open decisions)` section for the owner to decide, and a conflict stops the persist at the same Proceed / Refine / Abort ask (cannot ask ⇒ nothing persisted, `po_gate: needs_owner`). Plan Reviewer gains 17c. An AC, design or scope line that contradicts an applicable advisory rule needs an owner-acknowledged `Rule override: <id> — <why> (acknowledged by the owner: <where>, <date>)` line, or the brief gets a MEDIUM `rule_conformance` issue and a NEEDS_HUMAN decision. A scope line is never an override, and an override never relaxes a `must` rule. Launch Pad copies the section into the brief verbatim and owns the new human-only gate `LP-RULE-OVR` (`record-override` / `honor-rule` / `discard`). It never authors an override itself. When it cannot ask, it aborts with `rule_conflict_non_interactive`. This adds the gate's question-gate inventory row and lane-catalog entry, `test-rule-conformance-seam.sh` PART 5 with fixtures and three new mutation controls, and a `launch-pad` budget raise to 50965.
