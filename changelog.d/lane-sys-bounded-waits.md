Tell headless lanes what really happens to background work, so a wait loop cannot pin a lane
The fixed lane system prompt said "Ending your turn ENDS this process and stops any background task". That is false for a lane, which runs with `CLAUDE_CODE_PRINT_BG_WAIT_CEILING_MS=0`. L8's stream log from run automate-2026-10-15-163325 (2026-10-16) shows what actually happens. A foreground `until` loop that polled a finished subagent's transcript passed its 600 s Bash timeout and was moved to the background, not stopped. Its condition (the transcript's last line being an assistant message) never came true, because the last line was an `attachment` record. The lane then asked the owner and its turn ended `tool_deferred`, but the process waited on that task instead of exiting. When the coordinator killed the loop, the task's completion started a new turn that ended plain, which lost the question park and orphaned the question (retired since #574). `LANE_SYS` now states this behaviour and adds three rules: give every wait loop its own deadline shorter than the call's timeout; never poll a subagent's transcript or output files, and wait for its own result instead; and stop every background task before asking the owner. Tests H1b–H1f pin each sentence in the spawned prompt and check that the false claim is gone.
