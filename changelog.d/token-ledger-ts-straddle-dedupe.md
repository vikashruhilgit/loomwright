fix(token-ledger): the duplicate guard no longer lets a second-straddling pair of firings through
`test-token-ledger.sh` case 21a ("two CONCURRENT firings of one completion append exactly ONE line") kept failing on loaded CI runners with `expected='2' actual='3'`, and the lock was not the cause. `emit-token-ledger.sh` stamps `ts` per firing, before the per-log `mkdir` lock, so two firings of one completion that land either side of a second boundary differ in `ts` alone; the guard compared whole lines byte for byte, so it called them two events and appended both, with the lock serialising the compare correctly. A 15-way fan-out stress loop under CPU load reproduced it (3 of 20 rounds duplicated, every one with two distinct seconds and no lock timeout) and production hits the same path at the real fan-out. The guard now treats a last line identical except for a `ts` within two seconds as the same completion; the python step computes the neighbouring stamps, because BSD and GNU `date` disagree on parsing. A repeat further apart is still appended. New cases 21c–21e inject a day-crossing straddle through a `date` shim, require the duplicate back with only the window neutered, and bound the window at ten seconds. After the fix the stress loop duplicated in 0 of 20 rounds.
