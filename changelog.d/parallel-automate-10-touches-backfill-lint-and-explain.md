plan-waves --lint and --explain; every requirement writer emits Touches and Depends on

`automate-helpers.sh plan-waves` gains two read-only modes. `--explain` keeps the wave and blocked lines byte-identical and then names, for every placed item not in wave 1, each reason it stayed out of an earlier wave: the exact conflicting path and whether it was declared or added by a companion rule, the dependency it waited on, why its Touches is unknown (missing, the unparsable line and its number, or declared), a missing Depends on, a full wave, or a run-alone item ahead of it. `--lint <item|dir|runfile|item-list>` prints one line per item with `ok`, `ok (declared unknown)` or the exact bad line, its number and the reason for both sections, plus how many items will run alone, and exits 1 when any section is missing or unparsable; it reads the verdict from the same awk pass the planner uses, so lint and planner cannot disagree. Dismissed-finding drafts and the three `/propose` writers now emit `## Depends on` (`none`) and `## Touches` (the existing repo files the finding names, else `unknown`), and `user-story-writing` / Product Owner always write Depends on and fill Touches from the story's scope. The dismissed-finding and `--from-verify` extractors admit a named path into Touches only when it resolves to a regular, non-symlink file whose physically resolved parent directory lies under the repo root and outside `.git`, so a committed symlink cannot turn the existence check into a probe outside the checkout. The automate-loop skill documents the `--parallel N>1` intake lint contract for item 05; sequential runs are unchanged, and the backlog backfill stays an operator step.
