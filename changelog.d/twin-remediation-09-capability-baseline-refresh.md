Capability baseline refreshed by a real /capability-check scan
`loomwright/docs/CAPABILITY_BASELINE.json` had not been scanned since 2026-06-01 at plugin 14.6.0, and no scan report had ever been saved. A default `/capability-check` scan ran on 2026-10-12 under its 5-fetch budget, then `--update-baseline` recorded it: `baseline_date` 2026-10-12 and `plugin_version_at_last_scan` 15.132.0. The hook-event and frontmatter lists were re-derived from `hooks.json` and the 14 agent files and match. `tools_used` gains `Agent` and `TaskOutput`, and `model_tiers_used` drops `opus`, which no agent or spawn override uses. `features_used` adds the SessionStart `CLAUDE_ENV_FILE` export, the fail-CLOSED guards' PreToolUse deny, and Claude Mods via the sibling `loomwright-lanes`. `known_not_adopted` keeps its four entries with re-checked reasons; the `isolation: worktree` entry adds that the native worktree branches from the default branch, not the parent's HEAD. Coverage was partial, since the changelog was read only for v2.1.287 to v2.1.296, and the `_comment` says so. Nothing was adopted. The overlap map (twin-remediation/09 items 2 to 5) is out of scope.
