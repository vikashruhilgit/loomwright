Approach-challenged rounds are counted in the postmortem ledger
Every round `/pr-postmortem` categorizes now carries an optional boolean `approach_challenged`: true when the reviewer questioned the chosen approach, library, tool or mechanism rather than the code's correctness. Each new ledger line also carries a top-level `approach_challenges` count. It is not a seventh root-cause class: the round's `class` and `flow_stage` are unchanged, and `schema_version` stays 1. Older lines stay valid and read as unflagged. On a hit, `read-postmortem.sh` prints one more line right after the `self_heal_miss` line, `- approach-challenged rounds: N of M prior rounds`; it shows `0 of M` when nothing is flagged and nothing on a no-hit. The new `loomwright/scripts/postmortem-approach-baseline.sh` is a one-shot, read-only estimate over the existing ledger. It prints one TSV row per categorized round with a phrase-match guess, OR-ed with the same match over the PR's gathered review comments unless `--no-gh` is passed. It then prints an `approach_challenged: N of M categorized rounds (guess; …)` summary. A recorded flag always wins over the guess and is counted separately. The script writes nothing and always exits 0. The baseline number and the running-system evidence are in `docs/evidence/approach-baseline.md`.
