A stopped lane's timeline keeps its Supervisor phase
The `/lanes` timeline trusted a lane's Supervisor `state.md` only when `## Checkpoint` carried `created:` at or after the pick, or while the state read running and the lane still ran. Real state files do not carry `created:`: across 8 runs, 1 of 23 lane states had it, and the rest carry an agent-filled `last_checkpoint:` (a bare date, "(time not captured)", a fake midnight, the run's start time). So once a lane stopped (parked, stalled, died, removed), the timeline dropped its phase, its status and its Decisions Log rows. Identity is reliable where time is not: a lane runs one item in one session (lane-create writes a one-line backlog), and in 21 of those 23 states `## Session` `session_id` equals the lane's own session id, which the lane table records from the stream's `system/init`. `lane-status` now passes that id to `_lanes_timeline`. A state of the lane's own session is trusted like a running one, still with no time invented, whether or not the lane runs. Another session's state, a previous item's, is still never shown. A diff over every lane of 8 real runs changes no label, current step or start time; stopped lanes gain their `phase` / `phase_status` and Decisions Log lines. Tests AJ8n–AJ8q (mutation control included) and AJ11b–AJ11c through the real `lane-status` reader.
