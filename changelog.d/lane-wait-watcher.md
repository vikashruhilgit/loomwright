lane-wait — a coordinator lane watcher so a lane's question no longer sits unseen
A `--parallel` lane that needs the owner records its question and goes quiet, and nothing told the coordinator session: in wave W3 (2026-10-11) two lanes waited 22–24 minutes until the owner happened to look. The new `automate-lanes.sh lane-wait <parent_runfile> [--interval S] [--timeout S]` blocks, token-free, until a lane needs the owner (a new pending question, or a lane entering any state that is neither live nor coordinator-driven, so an unlisted run-file park such as `token_ceiling` wakes it too), prints one `lane-wait:` line per event and exits 0. The coordinator runs it as a background command so its exit wakes the session to relay the question, then re-arms it (automate-loop SKILL §14 step 8). A seen ledger (`<parent_run_id>.lane-wait-seen`) reports each question once and a park state once per entry. The watcher exits `idle` when no lane is running, never answers or launches anything, and writes nothing into a lane.
