Stories kept outside `.supervisor/requirements/` get their done stamp: `--source-requirement` from the `/automate` driver and `/autonomous` to Launch Pad
`/automate --folder stories` merged a story and left it unstamped: Launch Pad set `source_requirement` only for a goal path under `.supervisor/requirements/`, so the saved brief had no `Source requirement` line, `closeout` found no done brief whose pointer equalled the queued item, printed `closeout: skipped — no done brief`, and the story came back as an `AUT-LEFTOVER` that "Clean up now" could not clear. Launch Pad has a new explicit flag `--source-requirement <path>`: a relative `.md` path with no `..` segment that passes `test -f` is read as the requirement source and recorded as `source_requirement` exactly as passed, wherever it lives; anything else is ignored with a `source_requirement_unresolved: <path>` WARNING and the goal follows the old rule. The driver's `plan` step now spawns `/loomwright:launch-pad goal: <item> --source-requirement <item>` for every queued item (an item under `.supervisor/requirements/` resolves to the same pointer it always did), and `/autonomous` PLAN step 1 passes `<requirement_path> --source-requirement <requirement_path>`, so its Launch Pad forwards are now two. A hand-typed `goal: stories/x.md` stays a literal goal string. The guarded readers (`brief-pointer.sh`, `stamp-requirement-status.sh`, `reconcile-jobs.sh`) and `closeout.sh` are unchanged and still refuse a pointer outside `.supervisor/requirements/`; a new `outside-folder-story` case in `test-brief-pointer.sh` pins that. One behaviour change to know: a story with a `## Touches` section now reaches Launch Pad's Phase 6 scope check (`LP-SCOPE`) as `.supervisor/requirements/` items already did, so a brief planning outside it asks (or stops `scope_widening_non_interactive` under `--non-interactive`); a story without `## Touches` asks nothing. Pinned by the new `test-automate-next-step.d/15-source-requirement.sh`, a folder-story leg in `test-automate-trail.d/02-closeout.sh`, and the rewritten section (g) pins in `test-non-interactive-gates-seam.sh`.
