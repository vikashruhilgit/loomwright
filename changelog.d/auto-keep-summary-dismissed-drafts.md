Summary dismissed-finding drafts are recorded follow-up automatically, not asked
`/automate` no longer asks the owner about summary drafts of below-threshold dismissed findings (the owner kept them 185 of 194 times). `dismissed-drafts` now records every undecided summary draft of the item `follow-up` itself, through the existing `dismissed-decide` with a new trailing `--auto` flag that is accepted only with `follow-up` on a summary draft. It writes the same ledger rows and Decision rewrite as an owner's Keep, plus one `kept automatically` Progress line, and its output never displaces the count line. The driver's PICK `pending` step does the same for an undecided summary left by a run started before this change, interactive or `--non-interactive`. Per-finding drafts (`AUT-DISMISS` / `AUT-PENDING`) are asked exactly as before. The owner drops a kept summary by deleting it from `proposed/`; it is never recreated. `AUT-SUMMARY` stays in the gate catalog with unchanged labels, but only as the fallback for a summary whose auto-keep was refused (the ledger could not be appended to). That summary stays undecided, `dismissed-drafts` prints one `auto-keep refused` line before its count line, and the summary is asked at the decision step and the next PICK. An unreadable ledger still skips the whole pass and auto-keeps nothing. Known residual, unchanged rule: a finding listed in a kept summary that a later drain re-dismisses above the threshold inherits that summary's `follow-up` and gets no ask of its own. Before this change the summary was usually still undecided, so such a finding moved to its own draft and was asked.
