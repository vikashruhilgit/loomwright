Auto-answer routine question gates
The gate catalog (`lane-policy.sh catalog`) gains a third tier, `auto`, beside `allowed` and `human-only`: a routine gate whose catalog-designated answer (the row's one label marked `!`) is applied without asking, in sequential `/automate` runs, inline `/launch-pad`, phase sessions and lanes, unless a deterministic escalation trigger fires (`off`, `not_auto`, `label`, `destructive`, `severity` for an issue MEDIUM or higher, `high_risk` when `classify-risk.sh` reads true or null). The new `scripts/gate-auto.sh` holds the decision (`decide`), its one `## Progress` record per gate (`record`: `auto-answered <code>: <label> — <reason>` or `auto-escalated <code>: <trigger>`) and the block the owner reads at every park and at the run's end (`list`). Launch Pad Phase 6 consults it for `LP-SAVE` / `LP-SAVE-NOTE` with the PASS's highest issue severity and the brief's File Impact Map paths (read by the new read-only `classify-risk.sh --paths` mode; the refs mode and the merge gate's sixth condition are byte-identical); `/automate` intake consults it for `AUT-QUEUE` after `queue-confirm` asks; the driver decides `AUT-RESUME` and annotates its ask with `auto_answer` / `auto_reason`, which only a coordinator that could have asked applies. Moving `LP-SAVE-NOTE` out of `human-only` is a deliberate owner decision; it moved to `auto` only, so no project or wave policy ever answers it, while the `auto` codes that were `allowed` before stay policy-answerable exactly as before. Every can't-ask branch keeps its named status. Off switch: `--no-auto-gates` on `/automate` and `/launch-pad`, or `.supervisor/config.json` `{"auto_gates": false}`, persisted as `## Run Config` `- auto_gates: off` so a bare `--resume` keeps it and `lane-create` carries it into `lane.json`. Honest limits: in this repository most plugin-self briefs touch `agents/`, `commands/`, `skills/` or `hooks/`, so their `LP-SAVE-NOTE` still escalates `high_risk`; `AUT-QUEUE` takes no risk read because no item is picked yet.
