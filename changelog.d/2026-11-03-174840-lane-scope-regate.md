Launch Pad asks once about files outside an item's Touches
Launch Pad's save step now asks ONE question, under the new human-only gate `LP-SCOPE` (`Extend Touches` · `Revise plan` · `Stop`), listing every brief path outside the source requirement's `## Touches`, before the `LP-SAVE` / `LP-SAVE-NOTE` question. The list comes from a deterministic helper, `automate-lanes.sh scope-outside <item> <brief>` (new module `automate-lanes.d/scope.sh`), which reads Touches through the same `plan-waves --touches` call as the scope fence and wave-build's overlap check (companions included) and compares with the same matcher. It prints `inside`, `unknown` (Touches not declared: no question), `outside <n>` or `unreadable <n>` (the Touches read failed: every brief path is listed, never a silent pass). `Extend Touches` records the accepted paths in the brief's new `## Scope widening` section, and the save question that follows no longer mentions them. Files outside Touches are no longer a feasibility NO-GO. When Launch Pad cannot ask, it saves nothing and reports `scope_widening_non_interactive: <n> path(s) outside Touches`, even with the explicit non-interactive flag. Every Launch Pad question must name a catalog code; a free-form design question goes under `LP-CLARIFY`, never an improvised header (issue #808).

`/automate --parallel N` (N > 1) now re-checks lane overlap before any lane writes code. After a lane's Launch Pad phase saves its brief, the lane parks `scope_hold` (a new `pause_reason`; the item stays `running`) before its Supervisor phase. The coordinator runs `automate-lanes.sh lane-scope-check <lane>`, which compares the lane's declared set (Touches plus the saved brief's paths) with every other live lane of the wave in both directions, using the same reader and matcher as wave-build's overlap check. It prints `clear`, `overlap — <file> shared with <lane>` or `unreadable — <what>` (never read as clear). A clear lane is released with no question. Otherwise the owner gets ONE question under the new human-only gate `AUT-OVERLAP` (`Serialize` · `Continue in parallel` · `Defer to next wave` · `Revise`), answered through `lane-scope-decide`: `Serialize` waits for the other lane and merges its exact PR head first, `Defer to next wave` removes the lane and leaves its Queue row unchecked for the next wave, and `Revise` re-runs the Launch Pad phase with the shared files named. A lane's Launch Pad no longer asks `LP-SCOPE` itself. It records the widening as `- decision: pending-coordinator`, and the re-gate asks about it once. The hold survives the coordinator. `lane-status` (and `--json`, `--inbox`, the `/lanes` pane) shows it as waiting on the owner, and the coordinator's Stop hook blocks its turn end while a held lane has no decision. Single lanes, sequential runs and `--inline-phases` never hold.

The re-gate also holds up after a lane has moved on and after an owner answer that could never resolve. A sibling whose Supervisor already moved its brief out of `jobs/pending/` is still compared by name. `serialize` refuses a wait cycle or a lane that has ended, and a lane waiting on one that ends is asked about again. `Stop` at a relayed `LP-SCOPE` now checks the item off instead of re-queuing it. `--inline-phases` is refused inside a `--parallel N > 1` lane, where it could never hold. The shared brief reader now normalizes `..` and reads bare, single-quoted and trailing-commented entries, which also tightens wave-build's overlap check and the scope fence.
