A skipped test-integrity guard arm now leaves a durable `guard_arm_skipped` trace, and Supervisor reports `test_guard`
When the `PreToolUse[Agent|Task]` backstop (`guard-arm.sh arm-from-payload`) declines to arm for a Loomwright worker, execute-manager, supervisor-runner or review-pr-runner spawn, it appends one `guard_arm_skipped` row with reason `jq_missing`, `no_session_id`, `marker_write_failed` or `no_guard_dir`: to the session log when the id and log dir resolve, else to `arm-skipped.jsonl` beside the markers, else one stderr line. The exit code stays 0 and the hook keeps its `|| true`; non-armed roles stay silent. The Supervisor driver reads the marker and those rows at hand-over, and the completion tail prints `**Test guard:** armed | unarmed (<reason>) | not_applicable` in the job's `## Outcome` (`unknown (no_driver_handover)` on runs without a handover instruction).
