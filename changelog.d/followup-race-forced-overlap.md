The follow-up bundle lock's mutant leg no longer flakes
The test that removes `followup-bundle`'s lock and expects two concurrent bundles to both write left the overlap to the scheduler, so a loaded CI runner could run the two unlocked bundles one after the other in all three trials and the mutant read `1 1 1` and survived (seen on main at the #922 merge and on #939). `fu_race` now forces the overlap with a test-only PATH rendezvous: a racer that reaches the `mktemp` of the temp file beside the target (the last call before the write, after every check and after the render re-reads the draft) waits until the other racer is there too or is sleeping in the lock-wait loop. Without the lock both racers are inside check-then-act every time and the mutant reads `2 2 2`; with the lock the wait costs about 0.1 s. `followup.sh` is unchanged.
