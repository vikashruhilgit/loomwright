validation-gate fails closed on a Validation section it cannot parse
The shared Validation reader in `automate-lanes.sh` read only column-0 `N. ` and `- ` entries, so a `## Validation` section written with `* ` bullets or `N) ` numbering parsed to zero entries and `validation-gate` answered `none — no running-system step`, letting an unrun running-system step park READY. The reader now also accepts `* `, `+ ` and `N) ` entries, and a section that exists but still yields no entry produces one `unparsed` row, so `validation-gate` prints a step-less `unrun — <item>: the ## Validation section has no parseable entry (…)` (nothing to waive) and `lane-readiness` reads NOT-RUN. The PR-body not-run vocabulary now matches whole phrases only, so "the machine was not running hot" no longer turns an evidenced step into NOT-RUN. New legs AL22–AL22h in `test-automate-lanes.sh`, including a mutation control for the guard.
