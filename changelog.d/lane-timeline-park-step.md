Lane timeline keeps a pre-drain park on the step it reached
`lane-status --json`'s timeline (the `/lanes` step rail) read every `parked …` line as Merge evidence, so a lane that parked before its drain jumped to "Merge · step 7 of 7" with every earlier step drawn done: run automate-2026-10-15-163325 lane L5 stopped at Plan (Launch Pad Plan Review FAIL 3/3, owner chose Refine offline) and the pane showed Pick through Decide green. A park now reaches Merge only once the lane reached Drain; before that it joins the furthest step the other evidence shows, the lane state colors that step, and later steps stay `pending`. A `ran /autonomous` line with no PR URL that names Launch Pad (blocked, NO-GO, `launch_pad_blocked` — that wording is not pinned) and neither the Supervisor nor a saved brief now reaches Plan only, not Build. Regression cases AJ4b and AJ4d plus a mutation control (AJ4c) in `test-automate-lanes.sh`.
