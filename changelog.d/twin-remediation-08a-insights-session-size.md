/insights shows per-session context size, and the README teaches segmenting long sessions
The Token economics section of `/insights` gains a "Per-session context size" subsection (twin-remediation/08 items 2 and 3). It groups the transcript-byte proxy ledger lines by `session_id` and takes each session's largest recorded transcript, a maximum and not a sum, because the same growing transcript is snapshotted many times and a sum over-counted one session on this repo's ledger by about 200x. It shows the p50, p90 and largest values, the five largest sessions, and how many exceed an advisory threshold (`SESSION_CONTEXT_THRESHOLD_BYTES`, default 3000000 bytes, just under the p90 of the plugin's own ledger). When any session is over, it prints a one-line remedy: split at a natural checkpoint, catch up with `/handoff` in a fresh session, and resume with `/supervisor --continue` or a re-run of `/automate`. It never gates. With no ledger or no proxy lines it prints a no-data note instead of a zero, and sessions with only real-usage (token) lines are listed as not sized. The README's Workflow Tips gain a short "Segment long sessions" section. The fresh-session dispatch mode (item 1) and the cross-segment marker guard (item 4) are not part of this change.
